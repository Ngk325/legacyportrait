Questions

Is this written by AI?

We use AI to help draft the text from the facts you give us. You read and edit every word before anything is printed, and the portrait carries your byline.

Will it make things up?

It is instructed to use only what you tell us. Anything missing is left out, we check the draft against your answers, and you approve the final text.

What if my photo is old or blurry?

We warn you before you pay and suggest the best size. Many treasured photos print beautifully in black and white.

How long until I get it?

Digital is instant. Framed prints take up to about two weeks to produce and ship. Order by the date at the top of this page for holiday delivery.

Can I change it after I approve?

Yes, until your print is in production.

What if I'm not happy?

You approve the final text before we print, so we can't refund a typo you approved. If anything arrives damaged or printed wrong, we reprint it free. Request a refund or reprint within 7 days of delivery. Refunds and reprints

Is my family's information private?

Your photos and story are used only to make your portrait. See our privacy policy for how long we keep them.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We ship framed and printed portraits within the USA. Digital portraits work anywhere.
