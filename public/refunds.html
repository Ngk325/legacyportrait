Refunds and reprints

Draft. This page is a draft and has not yet been reviewed. It is not final.

You approve the final text before anything is printed, so here is how refunds and reprints work.

Within 7 days

  • Framed and printed portraits: ask within 7 days of delivery.
  • Digital portraits: ask within 7 days of purchase.

What we cover

  • Printing defects or damage in shipping: we reprint free. Please send a photo of the problem.
  • Lost parcels: we reprint free once the carrier confirms it is lost.

What we can't cover

  • Mistakes in text you approved (for example a misspelled name), because you read and approved every word before printing. We are happy to make a corrected copy at cost.
  • A change of mind once a made-to-order print is in production.

How to ask

Contact us (see our contact page) with your order number and, for damage, a photo. Refunds go back to the card you paid with.
