Privacy policy

This policy explains what we collect when you make a Legacy Portrait with The Legacy Press, why, who helps us process it, and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Your portrait material: the photos you upload and the answers, stories and text you give us about your family member.
  • Order details: your name, email address, shipping address and phone number when you order. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Basic technical data: your IP address and browser information, used to keep the site secure (for example, to stop automated abuse).
  • Marketing preferences: only if you tick the box to receive tips and holiday deadlines. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How we use it

  • To draft, show and print your portrait, and to deliver your order.
  • To send you emails about your draft and your order.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you opted in.

We do not sell your information.

Who processes it for us

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage of photos and drafts, and bot protection.
  • Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini, used as a backup): to draft and check the text from your answers. Your answers are sent to them only for that purpose. [VERIFY before launch: confirm each provider's API data terms, including that the data is not used to train their models, and that the Google key used is a paid, billing-enabled one.]
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Gelato: printing, framing and shipping physical orders.
  • Resend: sending emails.
  • Analytics and advertising (Google Analytics, Meta): only with your consent, when enabled.

How long we keep it

  • Unpaid drafts and their photos are deleted 30 days after they were last changed.
  • For paid orders we keep your original photos for 90 days after delivery, so we can reprint if something goes wrong, then delete them. The finished portrait and order records are kept as long as we need them for our accounts and the law.
  • You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time (see Contact). We will confirm when it is done.

Children

Our service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: see our contact page.
