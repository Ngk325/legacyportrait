Shipping

Draft. This page is a draft and has not yet been reviewed. It is not final.

  • Where: we ship framed and printed portraits within the United States, including Washington, DC. We don't ship outside the US yet. [APO/FPO addresses: to be confirmed with Gelato.] Digital portraits work anywhere.
  • Cost: standard shipping in the USA is free and included in the price.
  • How long: each portrait is printed and framed to order. Production and shipping usually take about 7 to 11 days, plus a day or two for our quality check. Digital portraits are ready immediately.
  • Holiday dates: we show an order-by date for the next holiday on every page. If that date has passed, you can still order: we'll send a printable gift card to give on the day, and the portrait ships right after.
  • Tracking: we email you a tracking link when your portrait ships.
  • Problems: see refunds and reprints.
