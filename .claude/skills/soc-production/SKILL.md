# Phase 3 — Production system

## Owner-time budget
Design for ≤ 2 hours a week of owner time: one 20–30 minute voice/video capture or brain dump, one review pass, and short daily engagement. Claude does the rest.

## Repurposing flow
One anchor piece per week (a newsletter, short video, voice note transcript, call recap, or case study) becomes: a long post, 3–5 short posts, a carousel or visual, a short clip with captions, a community answer and a newsletter section. Keep each derivative standalone and true to the source.

## Calendar (`social/calendar/YYYY-MM.md`)
Four weeks at a time: date · channel · pillar · format · hook · call to action · proof used · status (idea / drafted / reviewed / approved / scheduled / published). Rhythm over volume: choose a cadence the owner can sustain for 90 days.

## Templates (`social/templates/`)
Hook formulas that fit the voice; post skeletons per format; comment templates (add value, never pitch); DM follow-up for people who engage (staged, sent only with approval).

## Visuals and media
Canva for carousels and graphics; Higgsfield for short video; ElevenLabs for voice-overs only when clearly disclosed and in the owner's own voice with permission. Brand kit applied consistently. No fake people or fake screenshots presented as real. Follow platform rules on AI-generated media disclosure.

## Tool wiring
Postiz or the available scheduler (Ahrefs social post tools, Supermetrics social tools) for **draft scheduling**. Bitly for tagged links and QR codes. Google Sheets/Docs for the calendar and approvals.

Exit gate: first 4-week calendar built, repurposing flow tested on one anchor piece, templates and visuals ready as drafts.
