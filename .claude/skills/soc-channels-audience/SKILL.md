# Phase 1 — Channels and audience

## Pick channels from evidence
For each candidate channel (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook groups, X, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp channels/groups, newsletter/Substack, podcasts): where do the **buyers** and **referral partners** actually spend time? Evidence: where competitors and peers get real engagement, where buyers ask questions, community sizes, and the owner's existing audience. Use Ahrefs/Semrush social and brand tools and public-web research.

Choose **one primary and one secondary**. State why, and what will *not* be done for now. In Lebanon/Levant, weigh WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook; in US B2B and professional services, weigh LinkedIn, newsletter and local groups.

## Audience map (`social/audience.md`)
- The buyer and their trigger moments; the questions they ask; the words they use (voice of customer).
- Communities, groups, hashtags, creators and publications they follow.
- 50 **target accounts**: fit buyers, partners and amplifiers, tracked in `social/data/targets.csv` with why they matter. Public information only.

## Engagement plan
Meaningful comments on target accounts' posts (10–15 minutes a day), replies to relevant questions in communities, and warm introductions. Comment drafts are staged for the owner, never auto-posted.

## Channel setup checklist
Profile and bio that state who it's for and the next step; one tracked link per channel (Bitly); pinned post; consistent name and photo; contact method that matches the sales process.

Exit gate: channels chosen with reasons, audience map written, 50 target accounts started, profiles drafted.
