# Phase 4 — Publishing and community

## Publishing flow
1. Draft the week's batch as scheduled **drafts**; read each back from the tool.
2. `social-compliance-reviewer` (a different agent from the author) reviews every post: PASS / FIX / ESCALATE.
3. Open a PR or issue labeled `needs-approval-publish` listing every post with its exact text, media, link, time and proof used.
4. Only on an explicit yes naming the batch: set the approved posts live. Log in STATE.md. Anything not approved stays a draft.

## Community and replies
- Check comments and DMs daily. Route buying signals ("how much?", "can we talk?") to the owner or the sales process immediately, within the response-time promise.
- Draft replies in the owner's voice and stage them; the owner approves replies that speak for the business or touch pricing, regulated topics, complaints or people.
- Hide or report spam and abuse (free action). Never argue publicly; offer to take it private.

## Engagement routine (10–15 minutes a day)
Draft 5–10 thoughtful comments on target accounts and relevant community questions. Value first, no pitching in comments. Owner approves in a batch.

## Outbound DMs and connection requests
Only to people with a real reason and relevance; personal, short, honest. No mass DMs, no scraped lists, no automation that pretends to be the owner. Approval required for each batch.

## Regulated topics
If the venture touches insurance, lending, health, legal or investing: no coverage, financial, medical or legal advice in posts or replies; identify the licensed entity where required; check advertising rules for the line and market before publishing. Escalate with `needs-legal-review` when unsure.

Exit gate: first approved batch live, reply and engagement routine running, buying signals routed.
