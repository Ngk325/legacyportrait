# Phase 0 — Intake and voice

1. Read the repo, site copy and any `marketing/` or `sales/` files. Write `social/VENTURE.md` from `social/templates/VENTURE.md`. Mark each value **measured**, **owner-provided** or **proposed**.
2. Define the goal in one line: what conversation or action should content produce? (book a call, join a waitlist, reply with a question, subscribe).
3. Capture the voice in `social/voice.md`:
   - Source material: the owner's past writing, calls and notes. If a writing-style profile or brand-voice guidelines exist, use them (Idiolect, brand-voice plugin); otherwise gather 5–10 samples the owner wrote.
   - How they sound (sentence length, humor, directness), words they use and words they never use, topics they own, topics they avoid.
   - Do-not-say list and boundaries: personal or family matters, politics, competitors, clients, anything regulated.
4. Write three sample posts on different formats. Send them to the owner. **Do not proceed past Phase 0 until the owner says they sound like them.** Revise until they do.
5. Score social readiness 0–10 (clear buyer, clear offer, proof available, voice captured, time available) and name the three biggest blockers.
6. List at most 5 owner questions, each with your default. Open as issues.

Exit gate: `VENTURE.md` filled, voice guide written, three samples approved.
