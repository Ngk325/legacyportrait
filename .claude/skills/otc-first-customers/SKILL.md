# Phase 4 — First ten customers

The goal is ten people who paid, not a hundred who signed up. Work this in parallel with building.

## Who to ask first
1. People who already asked for it, complained about the problem, or got a quote and went quiet.
2. Friendly peers and past customers who trust the owner.
3. Communities and partners that serve the same buyer, through warm introductions.
Hand cold or outbound work to the Sales & Outbound kit; this kit supplies the offer, price and checkout.

## Pre-sale mechanics
- Offer the entry rung first: founding seat, paid pilot, deposit, or fixed-scope audit.
- Concierge delivery: do the work manually (spreadsheet, calls, hand-built output) and automate only what repeats.
- Be honest about what exists today and what is coming. Never sell vaporware as finished.
- Collect payment before or at the start of delivery, in line with the terms.

## Tracker (`cash/first-10.csv`)
name or alias · source · offer · price · status (asked / interested / committed / paid / delivered / asked-for-referral) · date · notes. Keep personal data minimal; real contact details live in the CRM.

## After each payment
Delivery check-in within 48 hours; ask what nearly stopped them from buying and what result they want; ask for a referral and, if they are happy, **permission** for a testimonial, in writing. Register approved testimonials in the claims register.

## Rules
- A paid customer is the only validation. Interest, likes and "send me more info" are not.
- Every sale updates `cash/STATE.md`; every lost deal logs a reason.
- Discounts need approval and stay inside the floor price.

Exit gate: design-partner and pre-sale plan live, tracker running, first outreach staged.
