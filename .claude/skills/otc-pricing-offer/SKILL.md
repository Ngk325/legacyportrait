# Phase 1 — Offer and pricing

## Offer ladder (`cash/offer.md`)
- **Entry offer:** the smallest paid step that proves the buyer's problem is real (paid pilot, audit, founding-member seat, deposit). Easy yes, quick delivery.
- **Core offer:** the main product with a clear outcome, scope and timeline.
- **Expansion:** upsell, premium tier, annual plan, add-ons, referrals.
Each rung: who it is for, what they get, what they don't get, price, delivery cost, and what proves it worked.

## Pricing (`cash/pricing.md`)
- Start from value and alternatives: what the buyer pays today (including in time), what competitors charge, what a win is worth to them. Cite competitor prices with date and source.
- Pick a model deliberately: flat fee, subscription, usage, per-seat, commission, or founding-cohort pricing with a stated cap and end date.
- Give a floor (never below margin), a target and a stretch price. Put the margin math beside each.
- Prefer fewer plans. Three at most at launch.
- **Founding cohort or scholarship pricing:** state the cap, the date, and what changes afterwards. Real scarcity only; never fake urgency.

## Guarantees and risk reversal
Write only what the business can honor. Define the exact conditions, the refund mechanic and who decides. Register the guarantee as a claim.

## Price tests (`cash/price-tests.md`)
Hypothesis · price points · audience · sample needed · decision rule. Early on, test by asking real buyers to commit (deposit, signed intent, paid pilot), not by surveys.

## Claims
Every outcome, number or comparison goes in `cash/claims-register.md` with source and status. Placeholders stay `[NEEDS PROOF]`.

Exit gate: ladder, price hypotheses with margin math, guarantee and test plan written; claims registered.
