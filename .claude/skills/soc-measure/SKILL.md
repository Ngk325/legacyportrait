# Phase 5 — Measurement

## What to track (`social/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, also in the Google Sheet)
- **Outcomes:** qualified conversations started, calls booked, signups, sales traced to content (tagged links plus "how did you hear about us?").
- **Quality signals:** replies and DMs from fit accounts, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks.
- **Reach context:** impressions, follower change. Treat as context only.
- **System health:** posts shipped vs plan, owner hours, approvals turnaround.

Pull from the platforms via Supermetrics, Ahrefs social tools, Bitly analytics and the CRM. Compute in code, not in your head; keep scripts in `social/reports/scripts/`.

## Reading results
- Compare formats, pillars, hooks and times, with sample sizes. A single viral post is not a strategy; look for patterns across at least 8–10 posts before shifting.
- Tag each post with pillar, format and hook so patterns are findable.
- Identify what produced conversations, not just likes.

## Decisions
End each report with **Do more of 3 · Do less of 3 · Test 1**. Propose new channels, paid boosts or cadence changes as approval requests. Retire pillars that produce no conversations after a fair test.

## Roll-up
Append one summary row (posts shipped, qualified conversations, bookings, owner hours, biggest risk) to the portfolio sheet if configured.

Exit gate: weekly loop running; at least four weeks of tagged data.
