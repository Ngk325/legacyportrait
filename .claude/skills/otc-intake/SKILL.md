# Phase 0 — Intake and economics

1. Read the repo and any `marketing/VENTURE.md` or `sales/VENTURE.md`. Write `cash/VENTURE.md` from `cash/templates/VENTURE.md`. Mark every value **measured**, **owner-provided** or **proposed**.
2. Identify what is actually being sold and how it is delivered: software subscription, service, coaching, product, license, lead fee, commission. Name the delivery cost and who delivers it.
3. Model unit economics (CLAUDE.md §7): price range, gross margin, fixed monthly cost, break-even customers. Put the spreadsheet in Google Sheets.
4. Name the **fastest realistic path to a first dollar** (who could pay this week, for what, how). If it needs more than 30 days, say what blocks it.
5. Identify the market(s) and the payment reality there (cards, bank transfer, WhatsApp-driven invoicing, cash). Note what must be verified in Phase 2.
6. Score monetization readiness 0–10: clear buyer, clear deliverable, price hypothesis, a way to take payment, terms, and someone to deliver. Name the three biggest blockers.
7. List at most 5 owner questions, each with your default. Open as issues.

Exit gate: `VENTURE.md` filled, economics modeled, first-dollar path named, readiness scored, questions logged.
