# Phase 2 — Pillars and proof bank

## Pillars (`social/pillars.md`)
Three to five themes the owner can speak about credibly and repeatedly. For each: the buyer problem it answers, the angle only this venture has, 10 post ideas, and the call to action it points to. A healthy mix:
- **Teach:** useful, specific answers to trigger-moment questions.
- **Show:** behind-the-scenes of building and working, with approved numbers.
- **Prove:** real customer outcomes and stories.
- **Take a stand:** an opinion the target buyer will recognize and discuss.
- **Invite:** the clear next step (reply, join, book).

## Proof bank (`social/proof-bank.md`)
Each entry: the story or number · source · date · who can be named · **permission status** (none / verbal / written) · where it may be used · expiry. Sources: customer wins, call notes, results, screenshots with permission, public milestones.
- Never invent or "round up" proof. Placeholders stay `[NEEDS PROOF]`.
- Customer names, faces and quotes need written permission; regulated industries may restrict testimonials entirely: check before use.
- Numbers (revenue, customers, results) need owner approval before posting and go in `social/claims-register.md`.
- Early on, the proof is the work itself: build-in-public updates, lessons learned, decisions and mistakes shared honestly.

Exit gate: pillars written, 50 post ideas banked, proof bank started with permission status on every entry.
